rclone-pi privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

rclone-pi is a personal, single-user setup of the open-source tool rclone on a home Raspberry Pi. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, rclone-pi uses the Google Drive API to read the files in the owner's Google Drive, and to create, read, update and delete the files that rclone-pi itself has uploaded.

How the data is used

Drive files are read so they can be opened on the owner's Raspberry Pi. Uploaded files are encrypted on the Pi before they are sent to Google Drive. Google user data is used for nothing else.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's Raspberry Pi. No Google user data is collected on any server, and none is sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the rclone configuration file on the Pi removes the stored token.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the app's Google sign-in consent screen.
